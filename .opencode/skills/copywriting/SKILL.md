## Landing Page Copy Rules

### Hero Section
- **Headline**: Focus on the primary benefit, not the product. Use "tu" (informal you) for connection. Max 8-10 words.
- **Subheadline**: Explain what it is + who it's for + key outcome. Max 20-25 words.
- **CTA**: Action verb + clear outcome. Examples: "Empieza Gratis", "Solicitar Demo", "Pre-inscribirme"
- **Never**: Lead with feature lists or technical jargon

### Benefits vs Features
- Always translate features into benefits:
  - Feature: "Integración con WhatsApp" → Benefit: "Tus pacientes te escriben donde ya están"
  - Feature: "IA conversacional" → Benefit: "Responde en segundos, no en horas"
  - Feature: "Agenda automática" → Benefit: "Citas agendadas sin que muevas un dedo"

### Social Proof
- Use specific numbers: "+500 citas agendadas" not "muchas citas"
- Include client names and clinic names when available
- Stats should be measurable and believable

### CTA Copy
- Use action verbs: Empieza, Solicita, Descubre, Únete, Reserva
- Create urgency when appropriate: "Cupos limitados", "Beta privada"
- Reduce friction: "Sin tarjeta", "Gratis 14 días"

## Blog Article Rules (Artículos)

### Structure
1. **Hook (first 3 lines)**: Use PAS framework — Problem, Agitation, Solution
2. **Answer-first opening**: LLMs and readers want the answer immediately
3. **Scannable body**: H2/H3 subheadings, bullet points, bold key ideas, max 3-4 line paragraphs
4. **Natural product placement**: Weave Vitalia in as the solution, don't hard-sell
5. **Internal links**: Link to related articles and `/estudios`

### SEO Constraints
- `description` in frontmatter: max 155 characters (enforced by Zod schema)
- Tags are required arrays of strings
- Content in Spanish

### Writing Framework
- **PAS**: Problem → Agitate the pain → Present Solution
- **AIDA**: Attention → Interest → Desire → Action
- **FAB**: Feature → Advantage → Benefit

## Case Study Rules (Estudios)

### PAR Structure
- **Problema**: What was the client's initial situation? Be specific.
- **Acción**: How did they use Vitalia? Describe the implementation.
- **Resultado**: Hard data only. "Aumentó citas en 35% en 2 semanas" not "mejoró mucho"

### Tone
- Objective and data-driven, not promotional
- Let the numbers sell — avoid adjectives like "increíble" or "asombroso"
- Include client name, clinic name, and timeframe when available

## Anti-patterns (avoid these)
- Generic claims without data ("el mejor software")
- Feature dumps without benefit translation
- Long paragraphs (break into 3-4 lines max)
- Passive voice — use active, direct language
- Exclamation overload — max 1 per section
- English idioms or Spanglish — keep it natural Latin American Spanish
